## Heavy metals in drinking water: health risk and DALYs
MS Research · 2023–2025 · KFUPM · Supervisor: Prof. Md. Shakhawat Hossain Chowdhury
{:.faded}

How much harm do trace metals in drinking water actually cause, and where? I gathered reported concentrations of inorganic arsenic, lead, mercury, cadmium, chromium and nickel and built probabilistic, multi-pathway exposure models that carry the uncertainty in body weight, intake and concentration through to the result. Monte Carlo simulation gives distributions of hazard index, cancer risk, lost disability-adjusted life years (DALYs) and their economic cost, and sensitivity analysis shows which factors drive the risk. A regional study of five Asian regions found lung cancer accounted for most of the burden everywhere, with the highest cancer risk in Southeast Asia and the highest population-adjusted DALYs in South Asia. A follow-up meta-analysis compares the burden across continents.

*Risk assessment · Monte Carlo simulation · DALYs · Python*

## NDMA exposure in Asian drinking water
2024–2025 · KFUPM
{:.faded}

N-nitrosodimethylamine (NDMA) is a carcinogen that can form during disinfection. We estimated exposure through ingestion, inhalation and skin contact during bathing, showering, swimming and cleaning. The average lifetime cancer risk came out at about 34 times the US EPA's 10⁻⁶ benchmark, and we estimated the DALYs and economic burden of the related bladder and liver cancers alongside strategies to control them.

*Disinfection by-products · Multi-pathway exposure · Risk mitigation*

## Inorganic arsenic in rice
2024–2025 · KFUPM
{:.faded}

A review of how rice takes up arsenic through silicon and phosphate pathways, how grain arsenic varies by region, and what it means for health. A probabilistic risk comparison ranked Asia highest, and the review weighs mitigation options from water management, soil amendments and biochar to nanoparticles and genetic approaches.

*Food safety · Arsenic · Probabilistic risk · Review*

## Extreme rainfall and climate projections in monsoon Bangladesh
2025–2026 · KFUPM
{:.faded}

A max-stable process framework for intensity–duration–frequency analysis that screened more than 2,500 model combinations across 35 stations, five seasons and durations from 1 to 48 hours. Local geography explained extremes better than remote climate teleconnections, and CMIP6 projections under SSP2-4.5 suggest rare 100-year events intensify by about 37% by 2050. A companion review examines physics-informed deep learning for flood inundation modeling.

*Extreme-value statistics · Max-stable processes · CMIP6 · Flood risk*

## Groundwater quality in Dhaka division
BSc Research · 2021–2022 · Islamic University of Technology · Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Md. Rezaul Karim
{:.faded}

Thirty-five years of groundwater data from 18 stations, 15 water-quality parameters. Mann–Kendall and Sen's slope tests traced long-term trends, GIS maps showed where they occur, and Piper diagrams characterized the water chemistry, revealing salinity problems at Madaripur Sadar and bicarbonate-dominated water across the division.

*Groundwater · GIS · Trend analysis · Geostatistics*
