## At a glance

My MS research at King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals quantified the human-health risk of heavy metals in drinking water worldwide. I use probabilistic exposure models, Monte Carlo simulation and disability-adjusted life years (DALYs) to turn contaminant data into clear measures of health and economic burden, and to point to the strategies that reduce it.
{:.lead}
